# reno-tahoe: Reno and Lake Tahoe venues (Cal Neva Lodge, Harold's Club, Harrah's Reno, Mapes Hotel)

## Ask
For each venue: confirm or correct opening and closing/demolition years; build its lineage (names, owners, dates); find sources on organised-crime ties, supporting and sceptical. Say if a label is unsupported. Never accuse living people.

## Cited findings
- Cal Neva: Giancana, on Nevada's List of Excluded Persons, was found at the Cal-Neva in 1963; Sinatra surrendered his licences for the Cal-Neva and Sands. — [KUNR](https://www.kunr.org/post/how-frank-sinatra-lost-his-nevada-gaming-license)
- FBI file records the licence surrender after Giancana was entertained at the Cal-Neva. — [FBI Sinatra file](https://archive.org/stream/SinatraFBI/sinatr4b_djvu.txt)
- Giancana called a guest of the licensed owner; Sinatra only "rumored" to be a front. — [UNOHP, Gaming Regulation in Nevada](https://archive.org/stream/GamingRegulation/GamingRegulation_djvu.txt)
- Sceptical: Sinatra denied inviting Giancana and said the property was not a mob front (1981 hearing). — [Las Vegas Sun 1998](https://lasvegassun.com/news/1998/may/15/allegations-of-mob-ties-haunted-the-chairman/)
- Cal Neva casino stopped April 2010; resort closed Sept 2013 for renovation. — [Sun 2010](https://lasvegassun.com/blogs/elsewhere/2010/apr/09/frank-sinatras-lake-tahoe-casino-shuts-down/), [AP 2013](https://lasvegassun.com/news/2013/sep/08/us-sinatras-casino-makeover/)
- Harold's Club: opened Feb 23, 1935; sold to Hughes June 1970; Fitzgerald Group 1988; Gamma 1995; closed Mar 31, 1995; imploded Dec 1999. — [Online Nevada](https://www.onlinenevada.org/articles/harolds-club), [Sun 1999](https://lasvegassun.com/news/1999/dec/14/harolds-club-in-reno-to-be-demolished/)
- Harrah's: bingo parlor 1937, Harrah's Reno Club 1946, Holiday Inns 1980; closed March 2020. — [Every Light Was On](https://archive.org/stream/EveryLightWasOnHarrahs/Every%20Light%20Was%20On%20(Harrah's)_djvu.txt), [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harrah%27s_Reno)
- Mapes: opened Dec 17, 1947; foreclosed Dec 1982; bought 1988; city 1996; imploded Jan 30, 2000. — [Online Nevada](https://www.onlinenevada.org/articles/mapes-hotel-and-casino-reno)

## Sceptical and alternative pass
- Cal Neva: Sinatra's denial (1981); ownership by Giancana appears only as FBI informant reporting and rumor, so that part is alleged. The Gaming Control Board action over Giancana's visit is documented.
- Harrah's: oral-history account that the company was not mob-owned (insider source). Harold's Club and Harrah's: found no accusation in any source opened; Kefauver index text search found no entry for Harold's, Harrah's or Cal-Neva (OCR index, weak). Mapes appears once in the index, context not opened.
- The one-sided caveat: labels "none" rest on absence in the sources I could reach, not on a clearance record.

## Corrections
- Cal Neva: "partnered with Giancana" is alleged, not documented; "closed 2013" is the resort (casino 2010).
- Harold's Club: not "family-owned throughout"; sold to Hughes 1970, Fitzgerald Group 1988, Gamma 1995.
- Harrah's Reno: closed March 2020, not null; casino dates from 1946 (1937 was a short-lived bingo parlor).
- Mapes: dates confirmed.

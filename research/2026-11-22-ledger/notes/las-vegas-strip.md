# las-vegas-strip: Las Vegas Strip venues

## Ask
For each of ten venues: confirm or correct opening and closing years; build the lineage (names and owners with dates); find sources on organised-crime ties in both directions; say where a label is unsupported.

## Cited findings
- Flamingo: Kefauver testimony (1950) has a witness say Lansky held some stock; Lansburgh and Cohen were later convicted of skimming it. Label documented. — [Kefauver Part 10](https://archive.org/stream/investigationofo10unit/investigationofo10unit_djvu.txt), [Online Nevada](https://www.onlinenevada.org/articles/flamingo-hotel)
- Desert Inn: Dalitz testified to the Cleveland group's controlling stake; staff summary ties him to the Mayfield Road gang. Label documented, but the claimed Gaming Control Board finding was not found. — [Kefauver Part 10](https://archive.org/stream/investigationofo10unit/investigationofo10unit_djvu.txt), [Online Nevada](https://www.onlinenevada.org/articles/desert-inn)
- Sands: Tax Commission refused Kufferman and Freedman licences; skimming and hidden-owner claims are reporting only. Label should be alleged. — [Online Nevada](https://www.onlinenevada.org/articles/sands-hotel), [Las Vegas Sun](https://lasvegassun.com/news/2008/may/15/mob-ties/)
- Dunes: front-man, Teamsters loan and investigator-belief reporting only. Alleged. — [Online Nevada](https://www.onlinenevada.org/articles/dunes-hotel)
- Riviera: Tax Commission licensing trouble over a Lansky-linked applicant; Greenbaum and Chicago partners reported. Alleged. — [Online Nevada](https://www.onlinenevada.org/articles/riviera-hotel)
- Tropicana: 1981 indictment and 1983 convictions for the Kansas City skim; Agosto guilty plea; Gaming Commission revoked two licences. Documented (Costello-era hidden ownership is only alleged). — [Online Nevada](https://www.onlinenevada.org/articles/tropicana), [Sun 1983](https://lasvegassun.com/news/1983/aug/30/joe-agosto-death-cripples-mafia-probe/)
- Stardust: skim traced to Kansas City, 1983 federal case, Rosenthal ban, 1984 commission fine. Documented via a secondary summary. — [Online Nevada](https://www.onlinenevada.org/articles/stardust-hotel)
- Caesars Palace: Teamsters loan and rumours; Control Board found no wrongdoing by the Perlmans. Label should be alleged. — [Online Nevada](https://www.onlinenevada.org/articles/caesars-palace)
- Circus Circus: investigators called Sarno a front man; a skim by Chicago interests reported. Alleged. — [Online Nevada](https://www.onlinenevada.org/articles/circus-circus)
- Bellagio: Gaming Commission approval 1998; nothing found tying it to organised crime. Label none. — [Sun](https://lasvegassun.com/news/1998/aug/21/wynn-gets-final-approval-for-bellagio/), [Online Nevada](https://www.onlinenevada.org/articles/steve-wynn)

Dates: all ten opening and closing years in the brief hold (Desert Inn imploded 2001 onward; Sands imploded 1996; Dunes closed Jan 1993; Tropicana closed April 2024).

## Sceptical and alternative pass
Sceptical material found: the Gaming Control Board found no wrongdoing by the Caesars owners, the Mirage inquiry found none by Wynn, and Kefauver testimony shows the Desert Inn stake was disclosed. Nothing sceptical was found for the Dunes or Riviera. Support for several venues rests on the Las Vegas Sun and Online Nevada rather than records.

# atlantic-city-nj: Atlantic City and Fort Lee venues

## Ask
For each venue: confirm opening and closing years, build the name and operator lineage, and find supporting and sceptical sources on organised-crime ties.

## Cited findings
- 500 Club: Kefauver Committee Part 18 (1950) has a witness naming Paul D'Amato as manager and another naming Marco Reginelli as head of the Atlantic County rackets. No Committee finding of Bruno-family control found. Opened as Phil Barr's 500 Cafe in the mid-1930s, closed by fire June 1973. [Kefauver Pt 18](https://archive.org/stream/investigationofo18unit/investigationofo18unit_djvu.txt), [500 Club](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/500_Club)
- Caesars and Bally's: the NJ Casino Control Commission says it found executives unsuitable and required their removal. [NJCCC history](https://www.njccc.gov/about/history/)
- Caesars: Perlman dealings with Alvin Malnik (a Lansky associate) per Wikipedia, so Lansky's circle, not the Chicago Outfit. [Caesars World](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caesars_World)
- Bally's: head William O'Donnell resigned over alleged mob links, which he denied. [Bally Manufacturing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bally_Manufacturing)
- Resorts opened 26 May 1978; the pages opened state a CIA-front reputation for Mary Carter, not a Lansky link. [Resorts International](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Resorts_International)
- Taj Mahal: reported 1992 Senate subcommittee report on an employee and Russian mob patrons. [Hard Rock AC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hard_Rock_Hotel_%26_Casino_Atlantic_City)
- Riviera opened 1931 (Ben Marden), reopened 1946 as Bill Miller's Riviera, closed 4 Oct 1953. [Riviera](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Riviera_(nightclub))
- Dates confirmed: Club Harlem 1935-1986; Resorts 1978; Bally's 1979; Caesars 1979; Harrah's 1980; Tropicana 1981; Trump Plaza 1984-2014; Taj 1990-2016; Borgata 2003.

## Corrections
- Riviera opened 1931, not 1930; name Bill Miller's applies 1946-53.
- 500 Club: 'documented' not supported by opened sources; suggest 'alleged'. Opening is mid-1930s.
- Bally's: 'none' contradicted by NJCCC and O'Donnell resignation.
- Caesars: 'Chicago Outfit' not supported; the named link is Malnik/Lansky circle.
- Resorts: Lansky link not found in opened sources. Trump Plaza and Taj: concrete-contractor claim not found.

## Sceptical and alternative pass
Kefauver Part 11 witness says he knew of no gambling at the 500 Club; D'Amato never convicted; a note says Reginelli was a patron, not owner. Nothing credible found against Harrah's, Tropicana, Borgata or Club Harlem, but those were checked only on Wikipedia.

## Gaps
- Court opinions (In re Boardwalk Regency, 90 N.J. 361), NJCCC decisions, NJ SCI reports, DGE 1987 report, 1992 Senate report, FinCEN penalties and Lansky/Resorts sources not opened: justia, leagle, courtlistener, dspace.njstatelib.org, senate.gov, upi.com and archive.org PDF downloads failed or were blocked.
- Fort Lee Riviera: no primary source. 17 of 25 candidates are Wikipedia (stance context only).

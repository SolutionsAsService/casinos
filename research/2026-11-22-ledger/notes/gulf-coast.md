# gulf-coast: Gulf Coast casinos (Biloxi, Harvey, Kenner, New Orleans)

## Ask
For each venue: confirm or correct opening and closing years; build the lineage (names and owners with dates); find sources on organised-crime ties, supporting and sceptical; say if a label is not supported by sources.

## Cited findings
- Casino Magic Biloxi opened June 5, 1993 (the company announced it in January 1993), not 1992; the barge was destroyed by Katrina in 2005 and the casino never reopened; Pinnacle passed it to Harrah's; the hotel is now the non-gaming Margaritaville Resort Biloxi (2016) — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Margaritaville_Resort_Biloxi)
- A 1998 report on Mississippi's first gaming regulator joining Casino Magic as an executive is a revolving-door story, not a mob story — [Las Vegas Sun](https://lasvegassun.com/news/1998/oct/28/controversy-over-ex-regulators-landing-casino-jobs/)
- President Casino Broadwater: opened August 1992, ended by Katrina in 2005 (record dates confirmed); history runs Broadwater Beach Hotel (1939) to President Casinos to Broadwater Development LLC — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/President_Casino_Broadwater_Resort)
- Boomtown Westbank opened August 6, 1994, renamed Boomtown New Orleans in 2002; Pinnacle from 1997 — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boomtown_New_Orleans)
- Treasure Chest opened 1994 with Boyd at 15%, Boyd full owner 1997 — [Wikipedia, Boyd Gaming](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boyd_Gaming)
- IP Biloxi opened December 22, 1997 as Imperial Palace; Boyd bought it October 2011 — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IP_Casino_Resort_Spa)
- Beau Rivage opened March 1999 — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beau_Rivage_(Mississippi))
- Harrah's New Orleans opened 1999 (temporary casino 1995), renamed Caesars New Orleans in May 2024 — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caesars_New_Orleans)
- Hard Rock Biloxi opened July 7, 2007 after Katrina; Twin River bought it in 2014; GLPI owns the real estate since 2023 — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hard_Rock_Hotel_%26_Casino_Biloxi)
- Golden Nugget Biloxi is the 1992 Isle of Capri casino, rebranded after a 2012 sale to Fertitta — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Golden_Nugget_Biloxi), [Isle of Capri Casinos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Isle_of_Capri_Casinos)

## Sceptical and alternative pass
Searched for Dixie Mafia, Mississippi Gaming Commission, Edwin Edwards riverboat licensing and Marcello ties. The Biloxi Dixie Mafia convictions (Halat) concern illegal vice and the 1987 Sherry murders, not these casinos. Louisiana riverboat licensing corruption (Edwards) is public corruption, covered only by Wikipedia pages I opened. Found nothing credible tying any venue to organised crime, so all are labelled none except Treasure Chest (unsupported, see gaps). Support for a mob label: none found. Every source opened is Wikipedia or a news report; no official source could be opened.

## Corrections
- casino-magic-biloxi: opened 1993 not 1992; closed 2005 (casino), not null.
- golden-nugget-biloxi: opened 1992 (as Isle of Capri), 2012 is the rebrand; the current summary describes the Las Vegas Golden Nugget (Guy McAfee, 1946).
- Treasure Chest label none is not source-backed: unsupported.

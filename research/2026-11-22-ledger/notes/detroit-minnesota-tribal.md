# detroit-minnesota-tribal: Detroit, Minnesota, Saratoga and tribal casinos

## Ask
For each of 12 venues: confirm or correct opening and closing years; build the lineage (names, owners, operators with dates); find sources on organised-crime ties in both directions; say where a label is not supported.

## Cited findings
- Saratoga Race Course: first meet 3 Aug 1863 on the Oklahoma Track; Waldbaum bought it in 1892, a Whitney group in 1901 — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saratoga_Race_Course)
- Kefauver Part 7 (full text) covers Saratoga lake houses (Arrowhead Inn, Piping Rock); a text search finds no Race Course, Canfield or Morrissey — [archive.org](https://archive.org/details/investigationofo07unit)
- Mob-era Saratoga gambling was at the lake houses (1930s to 1953), not the track or the Canfield Clubhouse — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saratoga_lake_houses)
- Canfield: Clubhouse begun 1866, main building 1870, gambling ended 1907, building now a museum — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canfield_Casino_and_Congress_Park)
- Canterbury: first race 26 Jun 1985; Ladbroke 1990; closed Dec 1992; Sampsons; Canterbury Park Jan 1995 — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canterbury_Park)
- Foxwoods: bingo 1986, table games 1992; 1996 report of a cheating scheme with crime-family associates, reported by the casino; 52 people banned — [Las Vegas Sun, 1996](https://lasvegassun.com/news/1996/apr/10/newspaper-report-some-dealers-implicated-in-organi/)
- Mystic Lake opened 12 May 1992 (Little Six Bingo 1982, Little Six Casino 1990) — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mystic_Lake_Casino_Hotel)
- Turning Stone opened 20 Jul 1993; compact and land disputes resolved 2013 — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Turning_Stone_Resort_Casino)
- Mohegan Sun opened 12 Oct 1996; Trading Cove Associates developer-manager — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mohegan_Sun)
- MGM Grand Detroit opened 29 Jul 1999, permanent 3 Oct 2007; MotorCity 14 Dec 1999, Ilitch took control 2005; Greektown 10 Nov 2000, sold in stages to Penn/Vici in 2019 — Wikipedia pages
- Pechanga opened 24 Jun 2002 — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pechanga_Resort_Casino)
- General: Senate testimony citing a 1988 Senate report that no proven organised-crime case existed in Indian gaming; later testimony that there was no substantial infiltration — [GovInfo 115-150](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/CHRG-115shrg28581/html/CHRG-115shrg28581.htm), [GovInfo 112-237](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/CHRG-112shrg72539/html/CHRG-112shrg72539.htm)

## Sceptical and alternative pass
- Saratoga: the supporting mob material (Piping Rock, Arrowhead) is about other venues. Nothing opened ties the track or the Canfield Casino to organised crime. Label none stands.
- Foxwoods: label moved to alleged on the strength of one 1996 wire report; tribe was the reporting party; no tribe or government is accused.
- Detroit: a 1999 wire report raised background concerns about the landlord of MGM Grand's temporary building (denied); Greektown's 2000 buy-out followed state background findings of unstated nature. Neither is an organised-crime finding, so labels stay none.
- Tribal venues: searches for Turning Stone, Mohegan, Mystic Lake, Pechanga and Mille Lacs found only cheating cases or nothing; general testimony is sympathetic and from interested witnesses.
- No source in this batch is a court record or gaming-board order.

## Corrections
- Canfield opening: gambling began 1866 (Clubhouse); 1870 is the building. 1907 closes gambling only.
- Foxwoods: "no organized crime involvement" overstated (1996 report); began as bingo 1986.
- Canterbury: closed for racing Dec 1992 to 1994.
- MotorCity: opened under Mandalay; Ilitch took control 2005.
- Greektown: sold in stages; 2000 buy-out followed background concerns about minority owners.
- MGM Grand Detroit: 1999 background concern about landlord.

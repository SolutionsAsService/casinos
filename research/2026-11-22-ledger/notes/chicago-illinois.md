# chicago-illinois: Chicago and Illinois venues

## Ask
For each venue: confirm opening and closing years; build the name and operator lineage; find sources on organised-crime ties in both directions.

## Cited findings
- Four Deuces opened 1919 (Colosimo and Torrio), not 1923; Capone worked there from 1919. [Colosimo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Big_Jim_Colosimo), [Torrio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johnny_Torrio), [Levee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Levee,_Chicago)
- Grand Terrace: Capone "controlled (or 25% controlled)" it per Hines's recollection; closing is 1940 on one page and 1950 on another. [Sunset Cafe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sunset_Cafe), [Earl Hines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Earl_Hines)
- Casino Queen opened 23 June 1993; Koman group to 2012, then an ESOP. [Casino Queen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Casino_Queen)
- Harrah's Joliet opened 4 May 1993; Hammons held the licence, Harrah's 80 percent, Vici from 2017. [Harrah's Joliet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harrah%27s_Joliet)
- Hollywood Casino Aurora opened 17 June 1993; the riverboat closed 10 June 2026, land casino opened 24 June 2026. [Aurora](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hollywood_Casino_Aurora)
- Grand Victoria Elgin opened 6 October 1994; Gold Strike/Hyatt, Circus Circus, MGM, then Caesars (2018). [Grand Victoria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grand_Victoria_Casino_Elgin)
- Kefauver hearings index lists Al Capone and a Capone Syndicate (index only). [Archive.org](https://archive.org/stream/investigationofo0112unit/investigationofo0112unit_djvu.txt)

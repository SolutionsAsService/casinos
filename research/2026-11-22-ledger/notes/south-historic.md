# south-historic: Historic southern venues (nine records)

## Ask
For each venue: confirm or correct opening and closing years; build the lineage (names and operators with dates); find sources on organised-crime ties, supporting and sceptical.

## Cited findings
- Colonial Inn: the Kefauver Part 1 hearing reads a tax return showing Joe Adonis (Doto) at 15 percent and says the casino ran through 1948, then became a burlesque house. [Part 1](https://archive.org/details/investigationofo01unit)
- Beverly Hills and Lookout House: Kefauver Parts 6 and 15 record the casino operations and Cleveland partners (Kleinman, Dalitz, Tucker, Rothkopf). Lookout gambling reportedly stopped in early 1951. [Part 6](https://archive.org/details/investigationofo06unit), [Part 15](https://archive.org/details/investigationofo15unit)
- Glenn Rendezvous: named as a gambling place run by Arthur Dennert (Part 15); Wikipedia gives the 1943 renaming and the 1948 sale to the Levinsons and Dennert. [Ed Levinson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ed_Levinson)
- Balinese Room: opened 1929 as Maceo's Grotto, renamed 1942, destroyed 2008. [Balinese Room](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Balinese_Room)
- Southern Club: Variety 1959 shows the casino operating; Hot Springs gambling closed in 1967. [Variety](https://archive.org/details/variety214-1959-04)

## Sceptical and alternative pass
Searched all 19 Kefauver volumes (OCR text) for Southern Club, Ohio Club, Vapors, Arlington, Madden, Balinese. No hit for the Ohio Club or the Vapors; the Arlington appears only as a hotel where Adonis and others stayed; the Southern Club only as "New Southern Club" in passing. So the claim that Kefauver named the Arlington or Vapors is not supported.

## Corrections
- Colonial Inn closed as a casino in 1948 (not 1957).
- Beverly Hills: not Shondor Birns in pages read; property continued to 1977.
- Glenn Rendezvous: name from 1943; operators Dennert and Levinsons, not shown as Cleveland.
- Arlington Hotel: no casino or 1967 closure shown; label cannot be documented.
- Balinese Room: 1942 was a renaming; opened 1929.

## Gaps
- Encyclopedia of Arkansas, TSHA, local Kentucky sources refused access; no Hot Springs court or state records opened.
- Ohio Club, Vapors: nothing opened.
- Closing dates for Lookout House, Glenn Rendezvous, Beverly Hills casino not found.
